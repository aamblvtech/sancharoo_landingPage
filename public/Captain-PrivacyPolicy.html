Sancharoo Captain Privacy Policy

Sancharoo is a ride-booking platform that connects customers with independent captains for Bike, Auto, and Car transportation services. Sancharoo operates on a unique model where captains pay only ₹20 per day and enjoy unlimited rides with 0% commission.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address
  • Date of Birth
  • Profile Photo
  • Residential Address

Identity Verification Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Driving License
  • Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Vehicle Insurance
  • Pollution Certificate (PUC)
  • Selfie Verification

Vehicle Information

  • Vehicle Type (Bike, Auto, Car)
  • Vehicle Number
  • Vehicle Model
  • Vehicle Color
  • Permit Details (where applicable)

Location Information

  • Live GPS Location
  • Pickup Location
  • Drop Location
  • Route Information
  • Trip History

Device Information

  • Device Model
  • Operating System
  • Device ID
  • IP Address
  • App Version
  • Network Information

Financial Information

  • Bank Account Details
  • UPI ID
  • Transaction Records
  • Subscription Payment Details

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage captain accounts.
  • Verify identity and eligibility.
  • Connect captains with customers.
  • Enable navigation and ride tracking.
  • Process subscription payments.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve app performance.
  • Detect fraud and misuse.
  • Ensure safety and security.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Subscription Model

  • Captains pay only ₹20 per day.
  • Unlimited rides during the active subscription period.
  • No commission deduction on completed rides.
  • Captains retain 100% of ride earnings.

4. Ride Data Collection

  • Ride ID
  • Pickup and Drop Locations
  • Route Information
  • Ride Duration
  • Distance Travelled
  • Ride Status
  • Timestamp Information

5. Information Sharing

With Customers:

  • Captain Name
  • Profile Photo
  • Vehicle Details
  • Vehicle Number
  • Ratings and Reviews

With Service Providers:

  • Payment Gateway Providers
  • Cloud Service Providers
  • Mapping and Navigation Services
  • Customer Support Platforms

6. Data Security

  • Encrypted Data Transmission
  • Secure Servers
  • Authentication Controls
  • Access Restrictions
  • Continuous Monitoring

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for account management, legal compliance, tax purposes, fraud prevention, and safety investigations.

8. Captain Rights

  • Access Personal Information
  • Update Profile Information
  • Correct Incorrect Data
  • Request Account Closure
  • Request Data Deletion (subject to legal obligations)
  • Contact Support for Privacy Concerns

9. Safety and Monitoring

  • Monitor Trip Activity
  • Verify Documents Periodically
  • Review Ride Reports
  • Investigate Complaints
  • Detect Suspicious Activities

10. Ratings and Reviews

Customers may rate and review captains after rides. Sancharoo may display ratings publicly, use feedback for quality improvement, and remove fraudulent or abusive reviews.

11. Fraud Prevention

  • Prevent Fake Accounts
  • Detect Unauthorized Access
  • Prevent Fraudulent Activities
  • Protect Captains and Customers

12. Third-Party Services

  • Google Maps
  • Payment Gateways
  • SMS Services
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers

13. Children's Privacy

Only individuals legally eligible to operate transportation services may register as captains. Persons under 18 years of age are not permitted to create captain accounts.

14. Changes to this Policy

Sancharoo may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
