Sancharoo User Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Sancharoo, a ride-booking platform that connects users with captains (drivers) for Bike, Auto, and Car rides. Sancharoo operates on a 0% Commission Model, ensuring fair and transparent services for both users and captains.

Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address
  • Profile Photo (Optional)
  • Gender
  • Date of Birth

Location Information:

  • Live GPS Location
  • Pickup Location
  • Drop Location
  • Travel Route Information
  • Trip History

Payment Information:

  • UPI ID
  • Transaction ID
  • Payment Status
  • Refund Details

Sancharoo does not store complete banking or card details.

How We Use Your Information

  • Ride Services
  • Safety Purposes
  • Customer Support
  • Service Improvement
  • Fraud Prevention
  • Security Monitoring

Location Tracking Policy

Location information may be collected before ride booking, during active rides, and until ride completion for navigation, fare estimation, ride tracking, and passenger safety.

Information Sharing Policy

Information may be shared with:

  • Captains (Drivers)
  • Payment Partners
  • Cloud Service Providers
  • Analytics Providers
  • Customer Support Platforms
  • Legal Authorities when required by law

Data Security

  • Data Encryption
  • Secure Cloud Storage
  • Access Control Systems
  • OTP Verification
  • Secure Payment Processing

User Rights

  • Access Information
  • Correct Information
  • Delete Information
  • Withdraw Consent
  • Data Portability

Account Deletion Policy

Users may request account deletion through:

  • App Settings
  • Customer Support
  • Email Requests

Communication Policy

  • OTP Verification Messages
  • Ride Updates
  • Transaction Notifications
  • Safety Alerts
  • Service Announcements

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies, Analytics Tools, and Session Technologies may be used to improve user experience, service performance, and security.

Children's Privacy

Sancharoo services are intended for users aged 18 years and above.

Safety and Emergency Features

  • Live Ride Tracking
  • Emergency SOS
  • Trusted Contact Sharing
  • Ride History
  • Captain Verification

Fraud Prevention Policy

  • Identity Verification
  • Suspicious Activity Monitoring
  • Account Restrictions
  • Suspension for Policy Violations

Data Retention Policy

Data is retained only as long as necessary for service operations, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Maps
  • Payment Gateways
  • SMS Providers
  • Analytics Platforms

International Data Transfers

Information may be processed on servers outside your region with appropriate safeguards to protect user data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Users will be notified of updates through app notifications, website updates, or email communications.
