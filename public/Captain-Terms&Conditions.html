Sancharoo Captain Terms & Conditions

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering as a Captain on Sancharoo, you agree to comply with these Terms & Conditions and all applicable laws and regulations.

2. Eligibility

  • Rider must be at least 18 years old.
  • Must possess a valid driving license.
  • Must provide accurate personal and vehicle information.
  • Vehicle documents, insurance, and permits must be valid and up to date.

3. Account Registration

  • Riders are responsible for maintaining account confidentiality.
  • Sharing accounts is prohibited.
  • False information may lead to suspension or termination.

4. Ride Acceptance and Completion

  • Riders may accept or reject ride requests.
  • Accepted rides should be completed professionally and safely.

5. Safety Requirements

  • Follow all traffic rules.
  • Provide helmets where required by law.
  • No alcohol, drugs, or reckless driving.

6. Payments and Earnings

  • Earnings are based on completed rides.
  • Applicable taxes are the rider’s responsibility.

7. Rider Conduct

  • Treat customers respectfully.
  • Maintain vehicle cleanliness and roadworthiness.
  • Avoid abusive or illegal behavior.

8. Cancellations

  • Avoid unnecessary cancellations.
  • Excessive cancellations may result in suspension.

9. Suspension and Termination

Fraud, policy violations, safety concerns, or submission of false documents may lead to account termination.

10. Limitation of Liability

Sancharoo acts as a technology platform connecting riders and customers.

11. Privacy

Rider information is processed according to the Privacy Policy.
