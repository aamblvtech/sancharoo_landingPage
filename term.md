Captains are independent service providers and not employees of Sancharoo. You retain full control over your work schedule, acceptance of rides, and routing. You are solely responsible for all operating costs, fuel, maintenance, and vehicle insurance.

### 2. Sancharoo Pass Model
Unlike traditional commission-based platforms, Sancharoo operates on a **Pass-based pricing model**:
* Captains purchase passes (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly) to unlock unlimited or specified ride matchings.
* Sancharoo takes 0% commission on the fares you earn while your pass is active.

# Privacy Policy - Captain

This Privacy Policy explains how Sancharoo handles the personal and location data of our Captains (drivers).

### 1. Background Location Tracking
To operate efficiently, the Sancharoo Captain app collects real-time location data:
* **Background Tracking:** We collect location data when the Captain app is open and your status is set to "Active" or "Online," even if the app is minimized or running in the background.
* **Purpose:** This is essential to assign nearby passenger bookings, calculate accurate routes, and ensure user safety. Location tracking stops when you go offline.

### 2. Documentation and Verifications
We collect and store sensitive driver information to comply with safety regulations:
* Government-issued identification (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.).
* Driver's license and vehicle registration (RC) details.
* Background verification certificates and vehicle insurance records.

### 3. Sharing with Passengers
When you accept a booking, we share your name, photo, vehicle license plate number, vehicle model, real-time location, and contact number with the passenger to ensure they can identify your vehicle.
